# Production pipeline review findings

1. **[P1] Preserve the current-only input policy during checkpoint selection and retrospective inference.** `src/gnss_field/training/prediction.py:41` constructs `ContextBuilder(store)` with its default `use_history=True`, and the evaluation contract at lines 43-45 contains no history policy. The pipeline trains the `current_only` chain through `_builder(use_history=False)` but sends mean screening, mean full-year selection, condition selection and D2020 inference through this evaluator. Thus the ablation is selected and, whenever its condition cache is unavailable, evaluated with historical values and masks restored. The C2019 condition cache can hide the error for final heads, while D2020 falls back to encoding the evaluator's history-enabled context. **Probe:** on the same synthetic packed hour, a current-only pipeline's training context had `0` nonzero elements in `coarse[:, 1:, :6]`; the context passed by `evaluate_predictor` had `15`. Pass the explicit history policy or the pipeline's context builder through every evaluation entry point and bind that policy into the artifact contract. Cover both mean selection and uncached functional D2020 inference in a regression test.

2. **[P1] Use an allocation-local clock for the engine wall limit.** `src/gnss_field/training/engine.py:208-210` compares the wall limit against cumulative `self.elapsed`, which `_load` restores from the previous checkpoint at line 140. `TrainingPipeline._engine` supplies `runtime.wall_hours * 3600` at `pipeline.py:158`. Once a stage reaches that limit, every resumed allocation exits before another optimizer update; promoted stages also inherit elapsed time from their quarter-budget source. **Probe:** save an otherwise valid step-zero checkpoint with `elapsed=10.1`, construct a fresh engine with `max_seconds=10`, and give it an objective that raises if called. The fresh invocation returns `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED`, `step=0`, with no objective call. Compare the current invocation's monotonic elapsed time against its allocation limit, retain cumulative elapsed separately for accounting, and keep campaign spending/attempt limits in the budget controller. Test that a fresh allocation advances after the previous allocation exhausted its wall limit, including a promoted source with substantial prior elapsed time.

3. **[P1] Keep seed and ablation products keyed by model family.** `src/gnss_field/training/pipeline.py:941-957` pools every advanced family into a single point/joint candidate list and selects one winner per product. The `pretrain_only` path repeats the same cross-family minimization at lines 985-988. `_evaluate_auxiliary` then evaluates only these winners at lines 1170-1178. When EDM and GAN both advance, one family's required repeat or ablation result disappears from D2020, and the remaining winner may change family across seeds or ablations. That comparison no longer isolates a seed or the named ablation factor. **Probe:** pass two trained point configurations, EDM with score 2 and GAN with score 1, through `_select_child_products`; its records contain both families, but `selected_products` contains only `{'point': 'gan'}`. Preserve selections by `(family, product)` through the lock, hash validation and D2020 output; select checkpoints within the locked family/configuration set. Add a two-family regression with opposite rankings across seeds/variants and verify that every required family reaches retrospective evaluation without changing family identity.
